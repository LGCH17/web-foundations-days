About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for jotting down ideas the moment they come to you. It keeps your notes in one place so nothing important slips away.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea into the "New note" box.
  2. Click "Add Note" to save it.
  3. Find your saved notes under "Your notes".

Features

  • Fast note capture
  • Live note count
  • Clean, distraction-free layout
